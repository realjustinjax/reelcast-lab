# Voice notes (default)

These are the neutral defaults. To set your own, copy this file to `Lab_Data/brand/voice.md` in your content folder and rewrite it in your own words. The editor reads it before writing any text (the post caption, the on-screen hook, the report).

- Write the way the speaker talks in the video. Plain words, short sentences.
- Say one thing per sentence. No hype, no filler, no promises the video does not make.
- Never invent facts, numbers, names or results that are not in the video.
- No em dashes or en dashes. Use commas, periods or parentheses.
- The post caption ends with a simple ask to follow or save, then 3 to 5 hashtags that fit the topic.
