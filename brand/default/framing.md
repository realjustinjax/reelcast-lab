# Framing notes (default)

These are the neutral defaults. To set your own, copy this file to `Lab_Data/brand/framing.md` in your content folder and describe your own setup (for example: "crop the top so the shelf above my head never shows").

- The speaker's face stays in the upper middle of the frame.
- Captions never cover the mouth or the eyes.
- Keep the whole raw frame unless these notes say to crop it.
